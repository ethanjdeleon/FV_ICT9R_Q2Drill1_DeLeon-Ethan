Beginner's Guide to Football⚽️

A basic guide on how to get started on football!



📋 Basic Rules

  • Two teams compete to score more goals than the other.
  • Each team has 11 players.
  • Only the goalkeeper can use their hands in their own penalty area.
  • No foul play.
